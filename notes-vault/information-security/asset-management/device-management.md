Process of controlling, configuring, monitoring, and securing the devices that connect to the organizations network and access its resources.  
Includes laptops, desktops, mobile phones, tablets, and IoT devices.  

### Mobile Device Management (MDM)
Lets organizations securely oversee employee devices, ensuring policy enforcement, software consistency and data protection.
Tools: Microsoft Intune, Jamf for Apple, VMware Workspace ONE  

### Mobile Application Management (MAM)
Manages only the corporate applications on the devices.  

##### Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
Permits employees to use personal devices for work.

##### Corporate-Owned, Personally Enabled (COPE)
The company provides mobile device to employees for both work and personal use.

##### Choose Your Own Device (CYOD)
Allows employees to choose devices from a company-approved list.

### Unified Endpoint Management (UEM)
Manages the entire device ecosystem of an organization from a single console.  
It is the single source trust from inventory and compliance.  
Tools: Microsoft Intune, VMware Workspace ONE, IBM MaaS360
