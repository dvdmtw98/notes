### Risk
Possibility that a threat will exploit a vulnerability and cause harm to the organization.  

**Threat**: Anything that can cause harm.  
**Vulnerability**: Weakness that the threat can exploit.  
**Impact**: What happens if threat successfully exploits the vulnerability.  

NIST Cybersecurity Framework places risk assessment in the Identify (first) function.

[[threats-and-vulnerabilities|Threats and Vulnerabilities]]

Risk = Likelihood \* Impact  

### Inherent Risk
The level of risk that exists in the environment before any controls are applied.

### Risk Management
Process of finding ways to minimize the likelihood of an undesirable outcome from occurring.  
ISO 31000:2018 is a widely used risk management framework.  

![[risk-management.webp|400]]

### Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
Essential predictive metrics used by organizations to signal rising risk levels in different parts of the enterprise  
Allows organizations to evaluate the impact and likelihood of risks, allowing proactive management to prevent their escalation
