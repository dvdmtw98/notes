### Security Policy
A formal, documented statement that defines the rules, expectations, and requirements for how an organization manages and protects its information and systems.  

Policies are the highest level of administrative control.  
They tell what **must** be done and **why**.  
The detailed **how** is left to standards, procedures and guidelines that sit below policies.

### Governance
System of rules, practices, and processes that an organization uses to direct and control its operations.  

[[governance|Governance]]

#### Board and Executive Level
Setting risk appetite, approving major security investments, and holding the CISO accountable for the security programs performance.  

#### Management Level
Translating executive direction into security policies and standards, overseeing compliance, and making resource allocation decisions.  

#### Operational Level
Following the policies and procedures that have been established, documenting actions, and escalating appropriately when something falls outside normal parameters.  

### Service-level Objectives (SLO)
A specific, measurable target that defines the expected performance level for a security activity or service.  

Defines Performance Expectations.  
Drives Prioritization.  
Create accountability between security teams and business stakeholders.
